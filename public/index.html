IMC-BOT

Offline

Connect

  1. Open discord.com/developers, press New Application, then Bot > Reset Token.
  2. On the same page switch on Server Members Intent and Message Content Intent.
  3. Paste the token here. It stays on this PC and is never shown again.
  4. Invite the bot to your server with OAuth2 > URL Generator (scopes bot and applications.commands, permission Administrator for the setup, you can lower it afterwards).

Your server

It only adds what is missing: roles (Mods, Tier 1/2/3 Sub), channels, a welcome and a rules message. It never changes or deletes what you have.

Switches

Moderation

Mods use /warn /timeout /untimeout /kick /ban /unban /unwarn /note /case /history /purge /slowmode /lock /unlock. Every action is a numbered case in #mod-log with an Undo button. Discord decides who sees each command: Server Settings, Integrations, IMC-Bot.
The warn ladder only acts for real when "Dry run" above is off. While it is on, #mod-log just says what the bot would have done.

AutoMod

Removes spam and other trouble from chat. A removed message becomes a warning from the bot, so the warn ladder above applies. Mods are never touched. With "Dry run" on, #mod-log only says what the bot would have done.

Regulars

New people are limited (no links, images or invites) until they have been in the server a while and taken part in real conversations. Then the bot gives them the Regular role and the limits go away. A conversation is a chat where the person and at least one other person both talk; after a quiet gap, the next chat counts as a new one. Mods are never limited, and mods can promote or hold back anyone with /regular. Members check themselves with /progress. With "Dry run" on, nothing is removed and no role is given; #mod-log only says what would happen.
LIMITED UNTIL REGULAR:

Twitch chat mirror

Shows your Twitch chat in #twitch-chat, under each chatter's name. Needs IMC-Stream running with the bridge. Nothing in a chat message can ping anyone on Discord. When Twitch deletes a message, times someone out or clears chat, the Discord lines go too.

Server log

Writes what happens in the server to #audit-log (mods only). Press "Set up my server" once to create that channel. Self-assign roles: a mod types /rolepanel in a channel to post a message with buttons.

Stream announcements

Posts in #announcements when a raid arrives and when you pass a round number of followers. Needs IMC-Stream running with the bridge. Viewers can also use /schedule to see your next streams.

Community

Commands for everyone: /suggest, /rank, /leaderboard. For mods: /poll, /giveaway, /suggestion, /ticketpanel, /rolepanel, /verifypanel. These need no setting. The three below do.

Discord alerts on stream

Shows Discord events as alerts on your stream, like a follow or a sub: a server boost, a new member, or a round member count. The look, sound and on/off of each alert are set in IMC-Stream (Alerts, and the Alert Builder), under "Discord server boost", "Discord member joined" and "Discord member milestone". Needs IMC-Stream running with the bridge.

IMC-Stream & go-live

Recaps made by IMC-Stream (Recap page) are posted in #announcements. Needs IMC-Stream running on this PC with the bridge (a version newer than 2026.10.17).
